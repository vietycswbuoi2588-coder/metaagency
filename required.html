Accept the invitation to join Meta Agency's business profile.

A business portfolio connects your Facebook Page, Instagram account, and other business assets so you can manage everything in one place.

When accepting an invitation to join a business portfolio, you must verify your identity by signing into Meta Agency using a Facebook account, Instagram account, or a Meta account managed by your organization.

Once you've joined the portfolio, depending on the access you've been granted, you'll be able to view and manage the business assets in the portfolio. If you have full control of this portfolio, you'll be able to assign and manage people's access to the portfolio and its assets.

Before you begin

  • You must have a Facebook account, an Instagram account, or a managed Meta account.
  • If your organization has created a managed account for you, you should use that account to join the business portfolio and sign into Meta Agency.

Accept an invitation to join a business portfolio

Accept the invitation

After accepting the invitation, you will be redirected to the Meta Agency homepage.

Note: If you join multiple business asset manager pages and sign into one page using a Facebook account and another using an Instagram or managed Meta account, the system will create a separate user page for each identity. To switch between these pages, use the page switcher tool at the top right of the Meta Agency homepage.
